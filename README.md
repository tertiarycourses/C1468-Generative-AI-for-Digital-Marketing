# C1468 — Generative AI for Digital Marketing

Use generative AI to plan campaigns, create content and accelerate your digital
marketing across channels. This two-day, hands-on course teaches you to use
generative AI — general chat assistants (**ChatGPT**, **Claude**, **Gemini**,
**Microsoft Copilot**) and **AI image and video tools** (DALL·E, Adobe Firefly,
Microsoft Designer, Gemini, Canva) — across the whole marketing workflow: setting
up an AI marketing toolkit and prompt library, defining a brand voice and audience
personas, generating responsibly, creating social, ad, email, image and video
content, keeping the brand consistent across channels, researching audiences and
keywords, planning a multi-channel campaign, personalising and scaling content,
A/B testing and optimising, analysing performance, automating workflows, reporting
insights, and assembling a reusable AI Marketing Playbook — while keeping the
strategy, the brand and the customer's trust firmly your own.

## One Connected Project

Every lab builds one **connected deliverable** — the **Neighbourhood Series launch
campaign** for Kopi Culture Co., a fictional Singapore specialty-coffee brand
releasing a range of local-flavour drip-bag coffees and a monthly subscription.
You start in Lab 1 by setting up an AI marketing toolkit and prompt library, and
across the two days take the campaign all the way to a complete, measured
multi-channel campaign and a reusable AI Marketing Playbook by Lab 16: a locked
brand voice and personas, responsible-generation rules, social, ad, email, image
and video content, a brand-consistency pass, audience and keyword research, a
multi-channel plan, personalised and A/B-tested content, a performance analysis,
an automation blueprint, a stakeholder report, and the playbook itself. A Kopi
Culture Co. brief is supplied in [`labs/reference-pack/`](labs/reference-pack/).

## Topics

1. **Getting Started with Generative AI for Marketing** — introduction to
   generative AI for digital marketing; popular GenAI tools (ChatGPT, Claude,
   image and video generators); writing effective prompts for marketing;
   generating copy, images and ideas. *(Labs 1–4)*
2. **Content Creation with Generative AI** — creating social media content;
   writing ad and email copy; generating images and videos; maintaining brand
   voice and consistency. *(Labs 5–8)*
3. **Campaign Planning and Optimisation with AI** — researching audiences and
   keywords; planning multi-channel campaigns; personalising and scaling content;
   A/B testing and optimisation. *(Labs 9–12)*
4. **Analytics and Workflow with AI** — analysing marketing performance with AI;
   automating marketing workflows; reporting and insights; building your AI
   marketing playbook. *(Labs 13–16)*
