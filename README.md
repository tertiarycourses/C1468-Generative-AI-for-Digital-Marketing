## Lab Activities

The 16 labs form one connected practical journey. Complete them in order so each verified output can support the activities that follow.

| # | Activity | Outcome |
|---:|----------|---------|
| **1** | [Set Up Your AI Marketing Toolkit](labs/lab-01-set-up-your-ai-marketing-toolkit.md) | Sign in to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and an AI image and video tool, run your first marketing prompts, and learn the generate–review–refine loop and where AI helps across the marketing workflow — the foundation… |
| **2** | [Write Effective Marketing Prompts and Build a Prompt Library](labs/lab-02-write-effective-marketing-prompts-and-build-a-prompt-library.md) | Turn a vague marketing ask into a strong, structured prompt (role, context, task, format, constraints), and save a reusable marketing prompt library for the work you repeat on every campaign. |
| **3** | [Define Your Brand Voice and Audience Personas](labs/lab-03-define-your-brand-voice-and-audience-personas.md) | Use AI to define a clear Kopi Culture Co. brand voice and to build the three audience personas from the brief — the foundation every later lab's content and targeting is held to — so the whole campaign sounds like one brand and… |
| **4** | [Generate Your First Copy, Images and Ideas — Responsibly](labs/lab-04-generate-your-first-copy-images-and-ideas-responsibly.md) | Generate a first batch of marketing copy, a starter image and campaign ideas for the Neighbourhood Series on your defined voice, and set the responsible-generation ground rules — accuracy of claims, originality, disclosure, data… |
| **5** | [Create Social Media Content](labs/lab-05-create-social-media-content.md) | Use AI to create platform-appropriate social media content for the Neighbourhood Series — scroll-stopping hooks, captions, hashtags and post concepts for Instagram and TikTok — organised into a first content calendar. |
| **6** | [Write Ad and Email Copy](labs/lab-06-write-ad-and-email-copy.md) | Use AI to write conversion-focused ad copy (Meta and Google) and launch and nurture emails for the Neighbourhood Series — headline and body variations, subject lines and clear calls to action — on the brand voice. |
| **7** | [Generate On-Brand Images and Short-Form Video](labs/lab-07-generate-on-brand-images-and-short-form-video.md) | Use AI image and video tools to generate on-brand visuals for the Neighbourhood Series — a moodboard, hero and lifestyle product images, and a short-form video script and clip for Reels or TikTok — with effective prompts. |
| **8** | [Maintain Brand Voice and Consistency Across Channels](labs/lab-08-maintain-brand-voice-and-consistency-across-channels.md) | Run an AI brand-consistency pass across everything created so far, and repurpose one core piece of content into every channel — so the whole Neighbourhood Series campaign sounds like one brand and carries one message. |
| **9** | [Research Audiences and Keywords](labs/lab-09-research-audiences-and-keywords.md) | Use AI to deepen the Neighbourhood Series audience research and to build keyword clusters for SEO and paid search — grounding the campaign in real customer language and search intent before you plan. |
| **10** | [Plan a Multi-Channel Campaign](labs/lab-10-plan-a-multi-channel-campaign.md) | Use AI to turn the research and content into a complete multi-channel campaign plan for the Neighbourhood Series — objectives, funnel stages, channel mix, content map and a realistic, budget-aware timeline and calendar. |
| **11** | [Personalise and Scale Content](labs/lab-11-personalise-and-scale-content.md) | Use AI to personalise the campaign message for each persona and segment, and to produce on-brand variants at scale — segmented emails, per-audience ad sets and localised captions — without losing the brand voice. |
| **12** | [A/B Test and Optimise](labs/lab-12-a-b-test-and-optimise.md) | Use AI to design and run A/B tests on the campaign's key elements — subject lines, ad headlines, hooks and CTAs — with clear hypotheses and metrics, then interpret the results and optimise with the winners. |
| **13** | [Analyse Marketing Performance with AI](labs/lab-13-analyse-marketing-performance-with-ai.md) | Use AI to analyse sample Neighbourhood Series campaign metrics — read the key numbers, diagnose what is underperforming and why, and decide the next action — while verifying the analysis and protecting data privacy. |
| **14** | [Automate a Marketing Workflow](labs/lab-14-automate-a-marketing-workflow.md) | Use AI to identify a repetitive marketing task and design a practical, repeatable automation for it — with a clear trigger, steps, a human review point and a brand-voice check — that your team could actually run. |
| **15** | [Reporting and Insights](labs/lab-15-reporting-and-insights.md) | Use AI to turn the campaign data into a clear, one-page stakeholder report — what happened, why, what it means and what you recommend next — in plain language, and save it as a reusable reporting template. |
| **16** | [Build Your AI Marketing Playbook](labs/lab-16-build-your-ai-marketing-playbook.md) | Assemble everything from the course into one reusable AI Marketing Playbook for the Neighbourhood Series and beyond, then structure and rehearse a short presentation of the campaign — the capstone deliverable. |

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- The accounts and software required for **ChatGPT · Claude · Google Gemini · Microsoft Copilot · Adobe Firefly · Canva**. Follow the setup and access notes in each lab.
- Review [`labs/tools.md`](labs/tools.md) before class for environment, account and safe-use requirements.
- A modern web browser and Git for cloning the materials.
- Synthetic or authorised data only. Do not place secrets, personal data or confidential material into an unapproved service.
- A named human reviewer for facts, calculations, decisions and any externally released output.

### 1. Clone the repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/tertiarycourses/C1468---Generative-AI-for-Digital-Marketing.git
cd C1468---Generative-AI-for-Digital-Marketing
```

### 2. Open the lab index

Start with [labs/README.md](labs/README.md), then complete Labs 1–16 in order. Each lab provides the activity context, practical steps and a way to verify the result.

### 3. Keep your connected outputs

Store each lab output in the suggested working folder and retain the evidence or review notes requested by the lab. Later activities depend on these approved outputs.
