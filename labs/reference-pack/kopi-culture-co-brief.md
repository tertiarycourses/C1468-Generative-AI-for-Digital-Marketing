# Kopi Culture Co. — Campaign Brief

> **This is a fictional brand created for the C1468 course.** All names, products
> and details are invented for practice. Use this brief as the source material for
> your Neighbourhood Series campaign, or substitute your own **non-confidential**
> brand or product.

## The brand in one line

**Kopi Culture Co. (KCC)** is a Singapore specialty-coffee brand — small-batch
roasts and **drip-bag coffee inspired by the city's neighbourhoods and local
flavours**.

## The brand story

- Founded **four years ago** by two friends who roasted their first beans in a
  Geylang shophouse kitchen.
- Known for **small-batch roasting**, everyday-easy drip bags, and flavours that
  nod to Singapore's kopitiam heritage without the gimmicks.
- A loyal following built through a small online store, weekend markets and a
  warm, witty social presence — not big advertising.
- The brand feels **proudly local, characterful, knowledgeable and a little
  cheeky** — it sells great coffee with a sense of place, not hype.

## The new launch — the Neighbourhood Series

A range of **four local-flavour drip-bag coffees**, each named for a Singapore
neighbourhood and its flavour, plus a monthly subscription — **The KCC Club** —
timed for the **year-end gifting season**. It is the brand's biggest release,
meant to win new customers and turn one-off buyers into subscribers.

| Coffee | Neighbourhood / flavour | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Tiong Bahru Gula Melaka** | Gula melaka (palm-sugar) note | Warm, caramel, comforting |
| **Katong Kaya** | Kaya (coconut-pandan) note | Sweet, nostalgic, smooth |
| **Geylang Teh Tarik** | Pulled-tea inspired, malty | Bold, milky, familiar |
| **Toa Payoh Pandan** | Pandan note | Fragrant, green, uplifting |

## The audiences

1. **Young urban coffee lovers (25–38)** — care about quality, origin and
   aesthetics; discover brands on **Instagram and TikTok**; want coffee with a
   story.
2. **Busy work-from-home professionals (30–45)** — want great coffee at home with
   convenience; value the **subscription** and consistency; time-poor.
3. **Gift shoppers** — buying a thoughtful **local present** for the year-end
   season; care about presentation, story and feeling generous.

## The channels

Organic **Instagram** and **TikTok**, a small **email** list, a **blog / SEO**
presence, a **modest Meta ads** budget, a **small Google search** budget, and
**WhatsApp** for existing-customer retention.

## The brand voice (starting notes — you define the full guide in Lab 3)

- **Tone:** warm, characterful, proudly local, a little witty — never corporate
  or hype.
- **Style:** short, vivid, conversational; concrete sensory detail; the odd
  Singlish wink, but always clear.
- **Voice:** a friendly neighbourhood barista who knows their coffee — invites and
  delights, never lectures or hard-sells.
- **We lean into:** the neighbourhoods, the local flavours, everyday ritual,
  small-batch craft and a sense of home.
- **We avoid:** clichés ("elevate your mornings", "the perfect gift", "game-changer"),
  hype, and any invented award, origin or health claim.

## The campaign goal (what the campaign must do)

Create and run the **Neighbourhood Series launch campaign** — social content, ad
and email copy, on-brand images and video, a multi-channel plan, personalised and
A/B-tested content, a performance analysis, an automation and a reusable
playbook — that **grows online sales and subscriptions** into the year-end season,
on a **modest small-brand budget**.

## Practical constraints

- **Modest budget:** favour high-leverage, low-cost moves (organic social, email,
  SEO, small partnerships) over big-spend media.
- **Season:** the launch lands in the **year-end gifting** window.
- **Responsible use:** do **not** invent statistics, awards, origins or health
  claims; do **not** imitate a real competitor's identity; keep customer data out
  of public AI tools; follow each ad platform's rules; be ready to disclose AI
  assistance where it matters (you set the rules in Lab 4).

## The look (starting notes — you set the full direction in Lab 7)

Warm natural light, **earthy warm tones**, marble and light-wood café surfaces,
subtle local textures (peranakan tile, kopitiam marble), and a modern, minimal
Singapore café feel. Everything should feel **small-batch and characterful**, not
mass-produced.
