# Kopi Culture Co. — Fact Sheet

> Fictional brand for the **C1468 Generative AI for Digital Marketing** course.
> These are the facts you reuse across the labs. **Use them as given — do not let
> the AI invent awards, origins, ratings or health claims that are not here.**

## Brand facts

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Brand** | Kopi Culture Co. (KCC) |
| **Category** | Singapore specialty coffee — small-batch roasts and drip-bag coffee |
| **Founded** | Four years ago, Geylang shophouse |
| **Sells via** | Small online store, weekend markets |
| **Personality** | Proudly local, characterful, knowledgeable, a little cheeky |
| **Season** | Year-end gifting launch |
| **Budget** | Modest small-brand budget |

## The Neighbourhood Series (the launch)

Four local-flavour drip-bag coffees plus a monthly subscription (**The KCC Club**).

| Coffee | Flavour note | Character |
|---|---|---|
| Tiong Bahru Gula Melaka | Gula melaka (palm sugar) | Warm, caramel, comforting |
| Katong Kaya | Kaya (coconut-pandan) | Sweet, nostalgic, smooth |
| Geylang Teh Tarik | Pulled-tea, malty | Bold, milky, familiar |
| Toa Payoh Pandan | Pandan | Fragrant, green, uplifting |

- **The KCC Club:** monthly subscription — a rotating selection delivered monthly,
  cancel anytime, member pricing.
- **Format:** premium drip bags (single-serve), sold singly, as a giftable set of
  four, and via subscription.

## The three audience personas (starting notes — you build the full personas in Lab 3)

| Persona | Age | Cares about | Discovers on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Young urban coffee lovers | 25–38 | Quality, origin, aesthetics, story | Instagram, TikTok |
| Busy WFH professionals | 30–45 | Convenience, consistency, value, subscription | Email, Google, Instagram |
| Gift shoppers | Mixed | Presentation, story, feeling generous | Instagram, word of mouth, search |

## Channels

Organic Instagram & TikTok · email list · blog / SEO · modest Meta ads · small
Google search ads · WhatsApp for retention.

## Brand voice cues

- **Use:** neighbourhood names, local flavours, everyday ritual, small-batch,
  warm and witty, clear.
- **Avoid:** "elevate your mornings", "the perfect gift", "game-changer",
  "must-try", hype, and any invented award/origin/health claim.
