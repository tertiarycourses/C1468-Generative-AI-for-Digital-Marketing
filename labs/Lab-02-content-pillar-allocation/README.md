# Lab 02: Content pillar allocation → Campaign economics

C1468 • v1.0 • Synthetic teaching scenario

## Scenario
Harbour & Grove is a fictional Singapore home-goods SME launching a reusable desk organiser. The retail price is S$39; estimated contribution margin is S$14 per order. No real customer or platform data is included.

## Deliverables
- four-week pillar mix; decision: Rebalance when a pillar misses its intended KPI.
- repeatable prompt template; decision: Keep facts and output schema explicit.
- claim-to-source register; decision: Hold unverified comparative or health claims.
- advocate engagement plan; decision: Use consented stories with attribution.
- budget guardrail sheet; decision: Stop spend above allowable CPA after sufficient evidence.

## Detailed procedure
1. Allocate a four-week content mix to educate, prove and offer; justify the share by journey need.
2. Use the prompt PDF to generate two drafts from the approved brief; compare each against the acceptance criteria.
3. Create a claim register with generated statement, source fact, reviewer and approval status.
4. Design a consent-based advocate request and a usage-rights record for customer material.
5. Compute allowable CPA from S$14 contribution margin and explain why ROAS alone is insufficient.
6. Save the approved prompt, rejected draft, claim register and economics sheet.

## Acceptance checks
- No real personal information or unlicensed customer content.
- A named source for every factual claim.
- One explicit reviewer decision per output.
- The mock campaign remains unpublished.
