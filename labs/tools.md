# Tools & Setup — Generative AI for Digital Marketing (C1468)

Everything you need for the 16 hands-on labs. Every tool used in this course is
**web-based and cloud-powered** — there is nothing to install beyond a modern
browser. You use two kinds of tool together: **general chat assistants** for the
research, planning, copy and analysis, and **AI image and video tools** for the
visuals.

## Accounts & software

| Tool | What it's for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Web browser** (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox) | Runs every tool in this course | Keep it up to date |
| **A chat assistant** — ChatGPT (chat.openai.com), Claude (claude.ai), Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Microsoft Copilot (copilot.microsoft.com) | Research, prompting, voice & personas, copy, planning, personalisation, testing, analysis, reporting, playbook | A free account for any one of them is enough; the trainer will confirm what's available |
| **An AI image tool** — DALL·E in ChatGPT, Adobe Firefly (firefly.adobe.com), Microsoft Designer, Gemini or Canva (canva.com) | Generating a moodboard and on-brand product and lifestyle images (Lab 7) | A free option is enough |
| **An AI video tool** — Canva, Gemini/Veo, or a free text-to-video generator | Assembling a short-form Reel / TikTok clip (Lab 7) | A free option is enough; the trainer will indicate what's available |
| **Optional: a keyword tool** — Google Keyword Planner or similar | Verifying keyword volume and competition (Lab 9) | Optional; AI estimates are confirmed here |
| **Optional: an automation tool** — a custom GPT / Claude Project, Zapier or Make | Exploring how to build a marketing automation (Lab 14) | Optional; the lab designs the blueprint either way |
| **Internet connection** | Every generative feature runs in the cloud | Required for every step |

## The AI workflow you'll use

Every lab follows the same loop — **generate → review → refine** — and the course
as a whole follows one workflow:

**Understand → Plan → Create → Optimise → Measure.**

You set the strategy, define the voice and personas, and bring the brand
judgement; the AI researches, drafts, visualises and analyses; you curate, verify
and own every result.

## Before Lab 1 — confirm your setup

1. Open and **sign in** to at least one chat assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot).
2. Open and **sign in** to an AI image tool (DALL·E in ChatGPT, Firefly, Designer, Gemini or Canva), and note an AI video option for Lab 7.
3. Send a simple prompt (for example *"Hello, are you ready to help me plan a digital marketing campaign?"*) and confirm you get a reply.
4. Create a project folder called **`KCC-Neighbourhood-Series`** for all your work.
5. Open the **Kopi Culture Co. brief** in [reference-pack/](reference-pack/).

If any of these is missing, tell the trainer at the start of the day.

## Responsible & accurate use

- Do **not** paste confidential customer data, personal information or unpublished plans into a public AI tool — use the supplied Kopi Culture Co. brief and sample data.
- Treat every AI output — **especially any statistic, claim or performance analysis** — as a first draft to review, verify and make on-brand. Never publish an unverified claim or number.
- Do **not** imitate a real competitor's protected identity, and follow the advertising rules of Meta and Google for any ad copy.
- Be transparent about AI assistance (including AI-generated images and video) where it matters, before anything goes to a real audience.
