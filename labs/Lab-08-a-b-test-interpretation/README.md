# Lab 08: A/B test interpretation → Organisational impact review

C1468 • v1.0 • Synthetic teaching scenario

## Scenario
Harbour & Grove is a fictional Singapore home-goods SME launching a reusable desk organiser. The retail price is S$39; estimated contribution margin is S$14 per order. No real customer or platform data is included.

## Deliverables
- test decision memo; decision: Do not crown a winner on tiny samples.
- reviewed weekly insight memo; decision: Every statement must trace to data cell.
- retargeting policy; decision: Exclude converters and honor consent changes.
- reallocation decision; decision: Reserve a test budget and cap downside.
- impact scorecard; decision: Adopt only with measured quality and governance.

## Detailed procedure
1. Write one A/B hypothesis, hold one variable constant and set a sample-size caveat.
2. Compare the two variants on primary conversion metric and lead quality, avoiding a decision from tiny samples.
3. Prompt AI to draft a weekly narrative from the synthetic rows; trace every claim to a cell.
4. Specify retargeting eligibility, consent window and converter suppression.
5. Allocate a small next-test budget using qualified return and a downside cap.
6. Compare baseline versus AI-assisted hours, approval quality, media outcome and review burden; save an impact memo.

## Acceptance checks
- No real personal information or unlicensed customer content.
- A named source for every factual claim.
- One explicit reviewer decision per output.
- The mock campaign remains unpublished.
