# Lab 04: Audience overlap and exclusions → Pilot test design

C1468 • v1.0 • Synthetic teaching scenario

## Scenario
Harbour & Grove is a fictional Singapore home-goods SME launching a reusable desk organiser. The retail price is S$39; estimated contribution margin is S$14 per order. No real customer or platform data is included.

## Deliverables
- channel reach plan; decision: Prioritize incremental reach over raw impressions.
- localization QA sheet; decision: Reject unsupported local stereotypes.
- funnel estimate; decision: Revise assumptions after pilot evidence.
- privacy-aware platform decision; decision: Avoid activating unconsented personal data.
- channel pilot plan; decision: Scale only after threshold and sample check.

## Detailed procedure
1. Estimate incremental unique reach after removing overlapping audience segments.
2. Review the Singapore-local draft for price, spelling, culture and unsupported stereotypes.
3. Calculate an opportunity funnel from reachable audience, response rate and lead qualification assumptions.
4. Identify personal-data categories in the draft tracking plan; mark the consent and vendor-control needed for each.
5. State a one-variable pilot hypothesis, test period, equal-budget rule and decision threshold.
6. Save the funnel, privacy gate and pilot approval memo.

## Acceptance checks
- No real personal information or unlicensed customer content.
- A named source for every factual claim.
- One explicit reviewer decision per output.
- The mock campaign remains unpublished.
