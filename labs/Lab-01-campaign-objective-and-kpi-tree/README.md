# Lab 01: Campaign objective and KPI tree → Campaign brief and brand voice

C1468 • v1.0 • Synthetic teaching scenario

## Scenario
Harbour & Grove is a fictional Singapore home-goods SME launching a reusable desk organiser. The retail price is S$39; estimated contribution margin is S$14 per order. No real customer or platform data is included.

## Deliverables
- SMART brief and KPI owner; decision: Choose lead volume only with quality guardrail.
- evidence-backed audience segment; decision: Reject personas without a source and confidence tag.
- journey-to-content matrix; decision: Every stage needs a testable next action.
- gap and differentiation map; decision: Adapt the unmet need, not the competitor wording.
- approved creative brief; decision: Approve only claims supported by source facts.

## Detailed procedure
1. Read the fictional product brief; list price, contribution margin, unsupported claims and campaign constraints.
2. In mock-data.csv, distinguish observed audience evidence from synthetic assumptions and mark confidence for each segment.
3. Write one business goal, one primary conversion event, baseline, target, owner and deadline.
4. Build a three-stage journey map with customer question, evidence and CTA for every stage.
5. Audit five synthetic competitor examples by hook, format, proof and CTA; record one unmet need.
6. Create a campaign brief with approved claims, prohibited claims, voice traits, target segment and review owner.
7. Save the KPI tree, journey map and reviewed brief.

## Acceptance checks
- No real personal information or unlicensed customer content.
- A named source for every factual claim.
- One explicit reviewer decision per output.
- The mock campaign remains unpublished.
